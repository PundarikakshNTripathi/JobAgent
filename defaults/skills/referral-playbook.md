# Referral playbook

1. Rank people by likelihood to answer, not by prestige: same team or adjacent team, individual contributor, 2 to 8 years in, recently active (posts, commits, talks), in the candidate's region or sharing a background (school, city, language), a mutual connection.
2. Order of attack: 1st-degree connection on the right team, then 1st-degree recruiter, then 2nd-degree with a mutual connection who can forward a two-line ask, then cold with a public hook (paper, repo, post).
3. First message asks a question, never "can you refer me". The referral comes after a reply, or for a 1st-degree connection who clearly works on the team.
4. One role per company. One person at a time; wait 4 to 7 days before the next or a single nudge.
5. Before messaging, do one visible thing that makes the message true: read the repo/paper, send a tiny PR or issue comment, post a measurement. The message then references it in one clause.
6. Never claim more than the evidence in the profile. Say what is in progress as in progress.
7. Timing: weekday morning in the reader's time zone; apply on the official form first or the same day so the referrer has something to attach to.
